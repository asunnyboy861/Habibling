Habibling Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Short version: Habibling collects no data. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no accounts. Your habits live on your device and in your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database. The only data that ever leaves your device is described below, and only when you choose to use it.

1. What We Collect

Nothing. Habibling has no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking frameworks, and no account system. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or usage statistics.

2. Your Habit Data

All of your data — habits, completions, notes, mood entries, and your pet — is stored:

  • On your device, in the app's local database (SwiftData); and
  • In your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit private database), used only to sync your data between your own devices. This data belongs to your Apple ID, is not readable by us, and is never accessed by the developer.

iCloud sync is performed entirely by Apple. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your device.

3. Optional iCloud Cloud AI (Habibling Cloud+ subscribers only)

If you subscribe to Habibling Cloud+, the app can generate AI-powered reports and messages (monthly deep report, weekly "Pixel Post", encouragement lines) using a cloud AI service. When you request these features, the app sends:

  • The names of your habits and their completion counts (e.g. "Read: 5 of 7 days");
  • A random, device-generated user identifier that is not linked to your Apple ID, email, or identity.

This request goes to our secure proxy (cramjam-api.calcs.top), which forwards it to the GLM large language model service and returns the generated text. We do not store your habit data on our servers. The proxy exists solely to keep the AI API key off your device.

You never have to use this. Cloud AI features are entirely optional. Without a Cloud+ subscription — or if you simply don't request AI content — nothing about your habits ever leaves your device. Habibling also offers on-device AI (Apple Intelligence, iOS 26+) and a built-in rule-based text engine, both of which process everything locally for free.

4. Voice Notes (Microphone & Speech Recognition)

Habibling offers optional voice dictation for habit journal notes. Audio is transcribed on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your recordings and transcripts are never sent to us and are not uploaded to any server other than Apple's on-device processing.

5. Feedback Form (Optional)

If you contact us through the in-app Support form, the app sends your message — and, only if you choose to type them, your name and email — to our feedback endpoint (msg.calcs.top). The full content is shown to you in the app before sending. You can always reach us instead by email (below). Support tickets are used solely to answer your question and are not shared with anyone else.

6. Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Habibling never sees your payment details. Purchase restoration happens directly between your device and Apple.

7. Children's Privacy

Habibling is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing to safeguard beyond what is described above.

8. Data Deletion

Delete any habit, note, or mood entry in the app and it is removed from your device and your iCloud sync. To remove everything, delete the app and (optionally) turn off iCloud sync for Habibling in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud. Because we hold no server-side data, deleting the app removes everything we could possibly have.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how data is handled, we will update this page and the app before the change takes effect.
