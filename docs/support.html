Quick Answers

What is Habibling?

Habibling is a pixel-pet habit tracker. Every habit you complete feeds your pet — a little 16×16 pixel companion that hatches from an egg and grows through 4 stages. Your pet never dies. Miss a day and it just takes a nap; your progress is measured by a forgiving "vibe %" score, never a guilt trip.

Getting started

  1. Download Habibling from the App Store — no account needed.
  2. Onboarding hatches your pet and helps you create your first habit (with an optional AI-suggested plan).
  3. Check in from the Today tab, or right from the home-screen pet widget.

How do I sync across devices?

Sync is automatic and free via your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud in iOS Settings and that Habibling is allowed under Settings → your name → iCloud → See All. No account inside Habibling is needed — we never see your data.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Data lets you export JSON (free tier) or CSV/JSON with import (Plus). Your data is always yours.

Voice notes aren't transcribing

Voice notes use on-device speech recognition. Check Settings → Habibling → Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed. Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone — nothing is uploaded.

How do I manage or cancel my Cloud+ subscription?

You can cancel anytime, in about ten seconds, and keep access until the end of the month you paid for:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find Habibling and tap it
  5. Choose Cancel Subscription (or switch plans)

Or manage directly on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Canceling stops all future charges immediately — you are never locked in.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Habibling → Settings (or the paywall) → tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase. Restoring is free and never expires — Plus is a one-time purchase, so it stays yours forever.

What happens if I cancel Cloud+?

Nothing dramatic. Your habits, pet, and stats are untouched. You keep Cloud+ AI features until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then the app simply falls back to the free on-device AI engine (rule-based text and Apple Intelligence where available) — all the same AI screens still work, just without the cloud model.

I paid for Plus but features are locked

Tap Restore Purchases first — this fixes it 99% of the time. Still stuck? Email us with your App Store receipt and we'll sort it out.
